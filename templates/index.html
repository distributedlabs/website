The Company OS

A Company OS that fits the company.

We bring a method we’ve used before. You know which approvals matter, where a job stalls and who quietly keeps it moving. Together we shape the workflows around those people. Here’s what that can look like.

01 / The brief

Write it down once.

Carry the client’s wording and decisions into the ticket. The developer picking it up on Monday can see what was agreed on Friday.

02 / Delivery

Give developers a change to review.

For repeatable software work, Hermes Agent can prepare a change, run your checks and open a pull request. Your developers decide what ships.

03 / Accounts

Get Friday’s update off the to-do list.

Draft the client update from work that actually shipped. The account lead checks it and sends it in their own words, without chasing the delivery team.

What happens after you hire us?

On the first call, bring one recent project. If there’s a useful place to start, we agree on the scope with you. Once you give us the go-ahead, here’s what your team can expect.

I

Trace a real job

At kickoff, the people who did the work walk us through it. We map the handoffs, pick one costly repeat task together and record how much hands-on time it takes today.

II

Try one change

We test a simpler workflow on a live project with the person who will run it. Where Hermes helps, we connect it to the tools you use and agree which steps need a human sign-off.

III

See what you got back

We compare time and rework against the starting point, including the effort of checking the output. Together we decide whether to keep the change, adjust it or stop.

What stays with your team

You get the workflow map and the before-and-after findings. For any change you keep, we walk the owner through running it, changing it and taking over manually. You can stop there or choose the next workflow with us.
